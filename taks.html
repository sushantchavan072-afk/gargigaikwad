Taks: Type Design

Type design

A modular Devanagari typeface

A structural, modular Devanagari display typeface

Taks is defined by sharp geometric cuts and a bold headline, inspired by the precision of carved forms and modular construction. The specimen brings together vowels, consonants, numerals and matra combinations in gold against a textured red-orange background.
