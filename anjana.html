Visual approach

Finding the story in a detail.

Person and place

The framing moves between close observations of the subject and glimpses of the surrounding market. A hand against a crate, the weight of a sack and a brief exchange become parts of the portrait.

Colour and texture

Orange and red fabric recur throughout the film, set against blue crates, shaded interiors and everyday surfaces. Close views of skin, woven cloth and objects give these small moments a tactile quality.
