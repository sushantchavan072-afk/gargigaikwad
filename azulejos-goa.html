Goa’s coloured tiles — a visual study of Indo-Portuguese azulejo heritage

The cobalt-blue tiles on Goan homes and churches are the subject of this illustrated publication. Gargi traces their Indo-Portuguese heritage through their patterns and motifs.

Editorial approach

Following the seams

The cover shows the joins between individual tiles. Seen up close, each tile holds a fragment of a tree, boat or building. Seen together, those fragments describe a single landscape.

Proposed design study

One tile, three scales

A fold-out spread could start with a single cobalt motif, repeat it into a border, then show the border at the scale of a wall. The reader could follow one shape from a detail on the page to the rhythm it gives a building.
