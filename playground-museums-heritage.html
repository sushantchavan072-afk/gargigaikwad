01 / Fort study

Rajgad

The publication is organised into history and architecture. Its opening chapters follow the fort’s origins, its role as a capital, sieges and the Mughal years. The architectural section introduces the Balekilla, the three Machis, gateways, bastions and water systems.

Orange chapter accents, photographic spreads and labelled plans help the reader move from a historical account to the physical form of the fort.

02 / Visitor guide

Pratapgad

A compact guide that combines a historical introduction with an illustrated fort map, important places to visit and nearby points of interest. Later pages bring together a suggested itinerary, visitor guidance and a photographic gallery.

Blue headings, numbered pages and yellow information panels give each type of content a clear place. The map and landmark descriptions sit together so readers can connect a location with what they will see there.
